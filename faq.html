🎵 Events & Tickets
What's included in my ticket price?
+
Your ticket includes 7 nights accommodation in your selected cabin type, all event access (main stage, sunset stage, sky lounge), all meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner), all workshops and activities on board, port entry fees, and welcome/farewell events. Alcoholic beverages are included in Voyager and Sovereign passes. Explorer pass holders can purchase drink packages separately.
How do I purchase tickets?
+
Tickets can be purchased directly through our website on the Events page. Select your ticket tier, complete your booking form, and pay securely via credit card, PayPal, or cryptocurrency. You'll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours with your digital ticket.
Can I buy tickets as a gift for someone?
+
Yes! You can purchase gift tickets. During checkout, select "This is a gift" and enter the recipient's email. They'll receive a gift code to claim their spot. Note that they'll need to complete their own profile and document uploads.
What if I want to upgrade my ticket type?
+
Upgrades are possible subject to availability. Log into your dashboard and request an upgrade from the Tickets section. You'll only pay the difference in price. Upgrades must be requested at least 30 days before departure.
Is there an age requirement?
+
DST events are for guests aged 21 and over. Valid government-issued photo ID will be required at check-in. No exceptions. This ensures the best experience for our adult community.
Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?
+
Ticket transfers are allowed up to 60 days before the event date. There is a $75 transfer fee. Contact our support team to initiate a transfer. Within 60 days of the event, tickets are non-transferable.
✈️ Travel Requirements
Do I need a passport?
+
Yes, a valid passport is required for all international voyages. Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the event end date. Passport scans must be uploaded to your dashboard at least 6 weeks before departure.
Do I need a visa?
+
Visa requirements vary by nationality and destination. For Caribbean voyages departing from Miami, US citizens and most EU/UK/Canadian citizens do not need a visa for the Bahamas. We recommend checking your country's official travel advisory. Our team can provide guidance for specific nationalities upon request.
Is travel insurance required?
+
Travel insurance is strongly recommended and required for all DST events. Your policy must cover medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and evacuation. Proof of insurance must be uploaded to your dashboard before boarding. Our preferred partner offers group rates — contact us for details.
How do I get to the departure port?
+
For Caribbean Odyssey 2025, the departure is from PortMiami, Terminal C. We recommend arriving in Miami the day before departure to avoid any travel delays. We partner with nearby hotels for discounted rates — see your confirmation email for details. Shuttle service from Miami International Airport is available.
Are there any health or vaccination requirements?
+
We follow all current health advisories. No specific vaccinations are currently required for Caribbean voyages, but we recommend standard travel vaccinations (Hepatitis A, Typhoid) and ensure you have any medications you need. Health forms may be required closer to the event date.
🛏️ Cabins
What cabin types are available?
+
We offer three cabin tiers: Explorer Bunk (shared, 4 guests, bunk beds), Ocean View Cabin (shared with 1 guest, queen bed, ocean window), and VIP Suite (private, king bed, balcony, butler service). Each tier is paired with a ticket tier — Explorer, Voyager, and Sovereign respectively.
Can I choose my specific cabin number?
+
Specific cabin assignments are made approximately 60 days before departure. While we cannot guarantee specific cabin numbers, you can note preferences (lower deck, starboard/port side) in your dashboard special requests. Sovereign pass holders get priority cabin selection.
Can I share a cabin with my friend?
+
Absolutely! Both guests must purchase the same cabin tier. Once booked, you can connect via the Roommates section in your dashboard. Send a roommate request and confirm the pairing. For Explorer bunks (4 guests), you can pair with up to 3 others.
What amenities are in the cabins?
+
All cabins include: air conditioning / climate control, in-room safe, daily housekeeping, Wi-Fi access, bathroom (shared for Explorer, private for Ocean View and Suite), and a welcome gift. Ocean View adds an ocean-facing window and priority Wi-Fi. VIP Suite adds a private balcony, butler service, Nespresso machine, premium minibar, and Bose sound system.
Is there luggage storage on board?
+
Yes, all cabins have closet and under-bed storage. Larger luggage pieces can be stored in the ship's luggage hold during port excursions. A recommended luggage size is one suitcase (max 50 lbs) and one carry-on per person.

💳 Refunds & Payments
What is your cancellation policy?
+
120+ days before event: Full refund minus $150 processing fee.
90–119 days: 75% refund.
60–89 days: 50% refund.
30–59 days: 25% refund (credit toward future event).
Under 30 days: No refund (ticket can be transferred to another person for a $75 fee).

We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance to protect against unexpected cancellations.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and major cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, USDC). Payment plans are available — pay 50% upfront and the remainder 90 days before departure. Contact us for payment plan arrangements.
What if DST cancels the event?
+
In the rare event that DST cancels a voyage (e.g., due to severe weather, port closures, or force majeure), all guests will receive a full refund within 14 business days OR the option to transfer their ticket to the next available event of the same type with a bonus $200 credit.
Is there a payment plan available?
+
Yes! We offer flexible payment plans. For events more than 90 days away, you can pay 40% upfront to secure your spot, 30% at 90 days before departure, and the remaining 30% at 60 days. Contact our support team to set up a payment plan after your initial booking.
📋 Event Rules
What is the code of conduct?
+
DST is built on respect, inclusivity, and good vibes. Our community code:

✅ Respect all guests and staff
✅ Obtain consent before any physical contact
✅ Report any concerns to our crew immediately
❌ No harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
❌ No fighting or aggressive behavior
❌ No photography/recording of others without consent

Violations may result in immediate removal from the event without refund.
What items are prohibited on board?
+
Prohibited items include: illegal substances, firearms and weapons, outside alcohol (beyond duty-free allowance), drones (unless pre-approved), flammable materials, and items that could damage ship equipment. All bags are subject to inspection at boarding. Confiscated items may not be returned.
Is smoking allowed?
+
Smoking (including vaping) is only permitted in designated smoking areas on the outer decks. Smoking is strictly prohibited in all cabins, indoor venues, and event spaces. Violation of this policy may result in cabin charges and event removal.
Can I leave the ship at port stops?
+
Yes! Port days are your time to explore. The ship will publish departure and return times for each port. You must return before the ship departs — we cannot wait for late guests. We recommend using DST-organized excursions to ensure you're back in time. If you miss the ship, all transportation costs back to the next port are your responsibility.
Is there a dress code?
+
There's no strict dress code — express yourself! However, some events have themes (details shared in advance). For fine dining restaurants, smart casual is requested (no swimwear or gym wear). The farewell gala has a "Sunset Glamour" theme — get creative! We celebrate self-expression and individuality.
