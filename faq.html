Booking
How do I book a ticket? +
Head to the Events page and select your preferred tier — Explorer, Voyager, or Sovereign. Click "Reserve Your Spot", fill in your details, and complete checkout. You'll receive a confirmation email with your booking reference within minutes.
How early do I need to book? +
We strongly recommend booking at least 8–12 weeks in advance. Cabin capacity is strictly limited and popular tiers sell out fast. Early-bird pricing is available for bookings made 3+ months before departure.
Can I book as a group? +
Absolutely. Groups of 6 or more qualify for our group rate — typically 10% off per person. Email groups@dontstoptripping.com with your group size and preferred dates for a custom quote. Groups of 12+ may receive a dedicated concierge.
Is there a waitlist if my preferred tier is sold out? +
Yes. Join the waitlist on the Events page and you'll be automatically notified if a spot becomes available — typically due to cancellations. Waitlist spots are offered first-come, first-served.
Do I need an account to book? +
An account is required to manage your booking, access digital tickets, complete cabin documents, and connect with roommates. Creating one takes under 2 minutes — head to the dashboard.
Tickets & Payments
What's included in each ticket tier? +
Explorer — Shared 4-berth cabin, all meals, festival access, open-bar pool deck.
Voyager (most popular) — Shared 2-berth ocean-view cabin, priority boarding, exclusive lounge, 1 shore excursion.
Sovereign — Private balcony suite, premium spirits, personal concierge, all excursions, artist meet & greet.

Full inclusions are on the Experience page.
What payment methods are accepted? +
All major cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfer for bookings over £1,000. Payments are processed via Stripe and are 256-bit SSL encrypted.
Can I pay in instalments? +
Yes. Our payment plan splits the total into 3 payments: 30% at booking, 35% at 90 days out, 35% at 30 days before departure. Select "Pay in Instalments" at checkout. No interest or fees apply.
Can I upgrade my ticket tier after booking? +
Upgrades are subject to availability. Log into your dashboard, go to My Tickets, and select "Request Upgrade". You only pay the price difference. Downgrades are processed as partial refunds per our cancellation policy.
When will I receive my ticket? +
Your digital ticket and QR boarding pass will be available in your account dashboard 30 days before departure, and emailed to you automatically. No physical ticket required — just bring your passport and QR code.
Onboard Experience
What food and drinks are included? +
All meals are included across every tier — buffet breakfast, sit-down lunch, and 3-course dinner nightly. Soft drinks, beer, house wine, and spirits are included at main bars. Premium spirits and champagne are Sovereign-only. Dietary requirements (vegan, halal, gluten-free) are fully catered — note these during booking.
Who are the artists performing? +
The full lineup is revealed in phases. Phase 1 (headline acts) drops 6 months out; Phase 2 (support) at 3 months; Phase 3 (full schedule) at 6 weeks. Follow @dontstoptripping on Instagram for announcements. Check the Events page for confirmed artists.
What is the dress code? +
Daytime: smart-casual resort wear. Pool deck: swimwear welcome. Evenings: smart-casual to cocktail — no sportswear. Themed nights (White Night, Gold Night, etc.) are announced in advance; participation is encouraged but optional.
Is there WiFi on the yacht? +
Complimentary WiFi is available throughout the ship, though speeds at sea can vary. A premium satellite package is available to purchase onboard for those requiring reliable connectivity.
Are shore excursions included? +
Voyager includes one curated excursion per port. Sovereign includes all excursions. Explorer guests can purchase excursions onboard or in advance via the dashboard at a discounted rate. Options typically include snorkelling, boat tours, cultural city walks, and beach clubs.
Travel & Documents
What documents do I need? +
A valid passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond the return date) is required. Some destinations may require a visa — check your country's requirements in advance. We send a full documents checklist 12 weeks before departure, trackable in your dashboard.
Do I need travel insurance? +
Travel insurance is strongly recommended and may be mandatory depending on the route. Your policy must cover cruise/sea travel, medical evacuation, and trip cancellation. You can add insurance at checkout or through your dashboard.
What port do we depart from? +
Departure port details are confirmed in your booking and visible in your dashboard. The 2025 voyage departs from Barcelona, Spain. We recommend arriving the day before — our concierge team can suggest partner hotels at embarkation.
What time is embarkation and disembarkation? +
Embarkation begins at 12:00 noon on departure day (Sovereign priority boarding from 11:00). Ship departs at 18:00. Disembarkation on the final morning starts at 08:00 and must be completed by 11:00. Exact times are in your digital boarding pass.
Cabins & Accommodation
Can I choose my cabin roommates? +
Yes. During booking enter the names/emails of people you'd like to share with — they'll receive a cabin invite. If travelling solo, opt-in to our roommate matching feature and we'll pair you with compatible travellers based on your preferences.
What are the cabin options? +
Explorer — Interior cabin, 4 berths, shared bathroom.
Voyager — Ocean-view cabin, 2 berths, en-suite.
Sovereign — Private balcony suite, king bed, en-suite, luxury amenities.

All cabins include daily housekeeping, fresh towels, and 24/7 room service.
Can couples book together at the Voyager tier? +
Yes. Voyager cabins are 2-berth, so couples book together and share their own private space. Add your partner during booking — both need a Voyager ticket. For a balcony and king bed, Sovereign is the ideal option.
What should I pack? +
Lightweight resort clothing, 2–3 evening outfits (smart casual), swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a light layer for cooler evenings, comfortable shoes for excursions, and your passport and documents. A full packing list is emailed 4 weeks before departure and available in your dashboard.
Policies & Cancellations
What is the cancellation policy? +
90+ days before departure: full refund minus £75 admin fee.
60–90 days: 50% refund.
30–60 days: 25% refund.
Under 30 days: no refund.

Refunds are processed within 10 business days. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
Can I transfer my ticket to someone else? +
Ticket transfers are permitted up to 30 days before departure for a £40 transfer fee. The new holder must provide full details and complete required documents. Contact support@dontstoptripping.com to initiate a transfer.
What is the minimum age? +
Don't Stop Tripping is strictly 18+. Valid government-issued photo ID proving age must be presented at embarkation. Anyone unable to prove they are 18 or over will be denied boarding with no refund.
What happens if the event is cancelled or postponed? +
Cancellation: full refund or free transfer to the next available event. Postponement: your booking transfers automatically to the new date. You may request a refund within 14 days of the postponement announcement if the new date doesn't work.
What is the code of conduct? +
DST is built on respect and good vibes. Threatening, harassing, or discriminatory behaviour will result in immediate removal at the next port at the passenger's own expense, with no refund. Zero tolerance for violence or illegal substance distribution.
