The Journey

An Unforgettable Voyage

The Caribbean Odyssey 2025 is our most ambitious voyage yet. Departing from the vibrant shores of Miami, we'll sail through the turquoise waters of the Bahamas, stopping at five exclusive destinations where luxury, music, and adventure converge.

By day, explore pristine beaches and crystal-clear waters. By night, lose yourself in world-class music performances as the stars light up the ocean around you. This is not just a trip — it's a transformation.
